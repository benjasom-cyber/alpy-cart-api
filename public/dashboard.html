A silent failure is a ticket the gatekeeper routed and that no flow answered, escalated, or put on hold. The customer is not abandoned — in season, routing assigns these to an agent anyway. By capability

Support-attributed revenue

Support-attributed revenue is every booking made within 30 days of a contact with us, whoever the customer was. It adds up two things that are deliberately kept apart everywhere else on this page: pre-sale (the customer had no booking when they contacted us — the support desk made the sale) and repeat (a returning customer who books again after writing in). The two rates are never added together, because a pre-sale rate and a repeat rate do not measure the same thing; the money is, because a booking is a booking.
Customers are matched on the md5 fingerprint of their email address — no address ever travels between Zendesk and the BI warehouse. Callers with no email on file are matched through the Odin phone index, fingerprint to fingerprint, never number to number.

Service quality · month by month

First reply is the time from ticket creation to the first public reply, as Zendesk itself measures it, reported as a median and a 90th percentile — never as an average, because one ticket answered after a week moves an average and tells you nothing. Satisfaction is good ratings ÷ answered ratings, the same definition Explore used, so the two agree. One touch is a ticket solved with a single public reply. Full resolution is wall clock from creation to solve — a service level, not time spent.

By agent · month in review

Revenue contribution is the booking value that followed a contact this agent took first — pre-sale and repeat together. It credits whoever answered, not whoever closed the ticket. Read it as influence, not commission: the customer may well have booked anyway, and a contact with no email and no matched phone number carries no revenue at all, so this is a floor, never a ceiling. Agents handling calls also show what their phone actually did: answered, missed and declined come from the Talk legs export, which counts every ring, not just the calls that connected.

Phone · monthly availability

Availability = inbound calls answered ÷ inbound calls offered during business hours. Calls outside opening hours are counted separately: they penalise nobody. Source: the Zendesk Talk incremental export, which is a real history — unlike the counters in the next section.
